# Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy

## 1. The Target User (The "Who")
Our exact target user is the **Head of Engineering** or **VP of Engineering** at a Series A to Series B startup (Team size: 20–80 people).

### Why this persona?
* **The Pain:** At this stage, they’ve just scaled their team. They moved from "everyone on one shared ChatGPT account" to "everyone has individual Cursor, Claude, and API keys."
* **The CFO Pressure:** The Finance department is starting to ask why the "Cloud & Software" line item is spiking by $2k–$5k every month.
* **The Authority:** They have the power to switch tools but are too busy to manually audit seat-counts and API tiers.

## 5. The Unfair Distribution Channel: The Credex Synergy
Since SpendsAudit AI is built to surface Credex for high-spend users, our unfair advantage is **Secondary Market Access**. 

* **The Differentiation:** Traditional audit tools only tell you to "cancel." We tell you to **"Swap for Credits."** * **The Magnet:** This makes the tool a lead-generation magnet for Credex’s core business, allowing us to offer the audit 100% free while competitors charge a % of savings.
