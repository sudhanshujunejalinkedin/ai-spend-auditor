# USER_INTERVIEWS.md

## Interview 1: Sagar Juneja, Freelance Web Developer
* **Status:** Freelancer / Solo Developer
* **Quotes:**
    * "I pay for both ChatGPT Plus and Cursor, but honestly I don’t fully use either of them."
    * "Sometimes it feels like subscriptions just keep running without any real reason."
    * "If a tool could clearly explain where I’m wasting money, I’d definitely use it."

* **Surprising Moment:**  
  He wasn’t focused on saving small amounts of money. His bigger frustration was not knowing which subscriptions were actually worth keeping.

* **Design Change:**  
  I added simpler explanations in the audit report instead of showing only numbers and percentages.

## Interview 2: Pratham Jain, Small Agency Owner
