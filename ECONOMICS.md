# ECONOMICS.MD: Unit Economics & Growth Strategy for Credex

## 1. Executive Summary: The Lead-Gen Engine
SpendsAudit AI is engineered as a high-intent filter for Credex’s financial ecosystem. By identifying businesses struggling with AI "leakage," we capture a segment of founders actively seeking cash flow optimization. These users are prime candidates for Credex’s credit and operational financing products.

## 2. Lead Value & LTV (Life Time Value) Reasoning
In B2B fintech, we don't look at single transactions; we look at long-term credit usage.
* **Average Customer LTV:** Estimated at **$5,000**. This is based on interest margins and recurring credit facility usage over 18-24 months.
* **Lead Worth Calculation:** We assume a **10% closing rate** from a consultation (booked after an audit) to a signed contract.
* **Qualified Lead Value:** Each audit user who books a call is worth **$500** ($5,000 * 0.10).

---

## 3. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) by Channel
We utilize three primary channels to drive traffic to the SpendsAudit tool:

| Channel | CAC per Audit | Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **X (Twitter)** | **$15** | Targeting 'Indie Hackers' and 'BuildInPublic' via niche ads and organic threads. |
| **LinkedIn** | **$45** | Direct outbound targeting of CTOs/CFOs using their specific AI spend as a hook. |
| **Product Hunt** | **$2** | Organic launch traffic; cost reflects time for asset creation. |

**Blended CAC Goal:** Weighted average of **$20 - $25** per completed audit.

---

## 4. Profitability Math
To be a sustainable lead-gen engine for Credex, the tool must convert users at the following rates:

1. **Audit Completed → Consultation Booked:** 15% (Users seeking a solution to identified leakage).
2. **Consultation → Credit Purchase (Closing):** 10%.
3. **Total Funnel Conversion:** **1.5%**.

**Breakeven Formula:**
$LTV \times Total\ Conv.\ Rate = Breakeven\ CAC$
$5,000 \times 0.015 = $75.

**Result:** As long as our CAC per audit stays below **$75**, SpendsAudit AI is a profit center for Credex.

## 5. The Road to $1M ARR in 18 Months
To hit $1M in Annual Recurring Revenue, the target is 200 active customers (at $5,000 annual value each).

* **Total Audits Required:** 13,333 completed audits (at 1.5% conversion).
* **Monthly Velocity:** ~740 audits per month.

### Requirements for Success:
* **Viral Loop:** The shareable report page must maintain a K-factor of 0.2 (every 5 reports trigger 1 new audit).
* **Market Relevance:** LLM pricing data (Cursor, OpenAI, etc.) must be updated monthly to maintain trust.
* **Credex Alignment:** The "Book Consultation" CTA must be the primary "fix" suggested on the report page.
